# Features

Every change to this app, and every change we have considered. One row per
change, in both tables.

- **`specs/<N>-<title>/`** is where a change is designed and built. The folder
  stays after it ships — it is the record, not scratch.
- **Implemented** is the archive: what landed, when, and what it produced.
  `/implement` writes the row.
- **Candidates** is the backlog: things worth building, with enough of a note to
  pick one up cold. A candidate is not a commitment and carries no number until
  `/brainstorm` allocates one.

Numbers are never reused. `N` for a new change is one higher than the largest
in either table.

| [1](1-project-scaffolding/) | Project scaffolding | 2026-09-17 | Next.js 16 + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + Vitest, deployable on Vercel defaults. D5 was graded **partly** and deferred to the first feature route: the guard allowed by default and named the three shapes it rejected, so a string reached through a local constant or an expression container walked past it. V3 was that route, and it made the hole wider than recorded — every word in the app moved into `src/features/panel/`, which the guard never read. A raw `Funky Drummer` planted in `Nameplate.tsx` on 2026-09-17 left 654 tests passing and lint at exit 0. | [2](2-a-reword-never-fails-a-test/) | A reword never fails a test | 2026-09-16 | `npm test` now runs two Vitest projects: `suite`, and `reword`, where `@/lib/snippets` resolves to a generated module whose every string has become `«reworded:notFound.body»`. A test that copied a word passes `suite` and fails `reword`, so the rule is proven rather than reviewed. A probe first measured that the rule held only by luck — the old string guard missed a partial quote and never read the repo root, and two planted violations sat in a green suite of 53 tests. `snippets.test.ts` is demoted to a fast advisory. Three verification rounds each found a live mutant — a fixture race, a gate that served real words while every test passed, and a one-line `include` that silently disabled it. 136 tests. |
| [3](3-the-front-panel/) | The front panel | 2026-09-17 | The skeuomorphic panel from `app2.png`, in dark and light, with every control a working dummy and no audio. Twenty-one design-system primitives across all five role folders, and the first feature slice, `src/features/panel/`. The look is a named surface vocabulary in `globals.css` — twenty CSS variables split into a metal set the dark block redefines and a lit set it never touches, plus thirteen `@utility` recipes — and no component may name a colour, write a hex, use a `dark:` variant or read `prefers-color-scheme`. Produced [ADR 0004](../docs/adr/0004-the-look-is-a-named-surface-vocabulary.md). **The build was green and the panel was flat**: nothing in the repo renders a pixel, so V3 added Playwright and `.shots/shoot.mjs`, and `docs/testing.md` now says to take the shot before calling a visual bullet done. Four reversals followed from looking: the fader drags along its own axis, "CSS gradients only" gave way to inline `feTurbulence` noise and real fonts, the knob and fader thumb became SVG geometry with knurled teeth and turned grain, and the corner screws went. None of them touched a component contract — components only apply recipe names. Also fixed: `snippets.test.ts` matched copies by raw substring, so `panel.autoFeel` read as a copy of "Feel" and the word "nothing" as a copy of "thin"; it matches whole words now and finally enforces ADR 0003's design-system half. 600 tests. Two bullets were graded **partly** and both closed on 2026-09-17: D1's untested `KnobFace` and `ThumbFace` had in fact been covered before the change landed and the report was stale, and D4's TAP TEMPO flash — asserted with `tone="amber"` while the panel passes `tone="steel"` — now has the steel pair tested at component level and the flash tested at panel level. The suite reads 642 tests rather than 654: `inlineWords.test.ts` asserts over the whole tree in three tests where `routes.test.ts` ran ten per-file cases, and a per-file case count moved with the lint fixtures the walk passes through. |
| [4](4-the-kit/) | The kit | 2026-09-17 | 24 mono Ogg Vorbis one-shots in `public/samples/`, 236 KB, covering all 17 articulations of `docs/music.md` Part 3 as real recordings — **zero substitutions**. MuldjordKit (CC BY 4.0) for 13 rows, VCSL and FreePats World Percussion (both CC0) for the cross-stick, pedal hat, cowbell and shaker, so the pack carries exactly one attribution line. `src/lib/kit/` holds the frozen `Voice`/`Articulation` contract and a `KIT` manifest a structural test holds against the folder in both directions. The loudness rule in `docs/music.md` turned out to be unbuildable and was replaced mid-build: EBU R128 integrates over 400 ms behind a −70 LUFS gate, so a 100 ms rimshot reports the gate floor, and padding or momentary-max both read a short sample quieter than a long one at the same amplitude. Normalization is now onset-anchored RMS — −21.0 dBFS over 100 ms from the onset, target derived as `−1 dBFS − worst crest` — measured across the pack at −21.10 to −20.91. Produced [ADR 0005](../docs/adr/0005-samples-are-normalized-to-onset-window-rms.md) and [ADR 0006](../docs/adr/0006-the-kit-ships-as-ogg-vorbis-only.md), and amended `docs/music.md` twice. The musician found `Snare1`/`Snare2` correlate at r = −0.30 with identical lengths — top and bottom mic of one performance, not two takes — so alternating them as a round robin would change microphone every note; all four snare articulations come from `Snare1`. 654 tests. D1 was closed by ear on 2026-09-17: the backbeat does not crack and the kit is accepted without a rimshot, the four imports sit in the room, and `hihat.pedal` reads as a stick rather than a foot — left in place and carried as a candidate. |
| [5](5-the-first-beat/) | The first beat | 2026-09-17 | The app makes a sound. `globalTime` and `baseBeat` are the first two stages of a pipeline whose stage list is deliberately open — every stage is `(bar, ctx) => bar`, `PIPELINE` is an ordered array, and adding one is a file plus a line ([ADR 0007](../docs/adr/0007-the-drummer-is-a-pipeline-of-same-signature-stages.md)). Under them: ADR 0001's grid parser, the ten grids of `docs/music.md` Part 2 as data, a sample player over V4's kit, and a 100 ms-lookahead scheduler on the audio clock. Play/Stop and Tempo are live; swing, Feel, the mutes and the auto- buttons stay dummies, and the app plays dead straight until the swing stage lands. The purity rule in `docs/coding-guidelines.md` decided the shape: the whole drummer is pure and sits in `src/lib/`, the `AudioContext` and the loop are a new `src/features/transport/` slice, and because no feature may import a feature, `src/app/page.tsx` composes the two and `tsc` is the only thing holding `PanelControls` and `TransportControls` in agreement ([ADR 0008](../docs/adr/0008-the-drummer-is-domain-the-transport-is-a-slice.md)). `usePanelState` is gone and `Panel` is fully controlled. The `musician` wrote the missing grid→articulation map into `docs/music.md` Part 3 and found that Four On The Floor opens the hat on every upbeat and writes nothing after it, so Part 3's "always closed on the next grid step" cannot be met by notation and the choke group is required rather than optional. Two criteria were proven by experiment rather than asserted: moving a note and changing a level in a real groove left all 334 `src/lib/` tests green, and appending a real third stage took `pipeline.test.ts` from 37 tests to 42 with no edit to the file. The verifier also caught `swing` carrying two units under one name — a percentage on a groove, a ratio in the time module — which was closed by making it a percentage everywhere. **D1 was settled by ear the same day** — Fred: "it sounds good" — with one change asked for on hearing it, and made before commit: tempo and Stop took a whole bar to land, because the scheduler committed a bar to the audio clock the moment its bar line entered the 100 ms window. Pressing Stop left 2.5 s of drumming at 96 BPM. It now commits **one beat at a time**, so both land inside a beat; Stop lets the committed beat finish and lets an open hat ring out rather than cutting it. The pipeline still computes a bar — velocity curves and fills are bar-shaped — but `BarContext` anchors on an absolute beat, so a tempo change re-anchors mid-bar and no stage sees a difference. `docs/music.md`'s binding "takes effect at the next bar line" and ADR 0007 were both amended. 1022 tests. |
