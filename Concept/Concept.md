Funky Drummer

* a metronome / groove box that is only playing funk
* the drummer adds variations and fills, so the player can hear how a groove can be played
* there are 8 drum instruments: Kick, Snare, Hi-hat, Ride, Cowbell, shaker, toms and crash
* each instrument can be muted.
* when an instrument is muted, the other available instruments fill up the groove
* the drummer starts from hand selected 1-2 bar grooves (randomly selected)
* drummer adds a velocity curve per bar
* drummer adds a velocity curve per 4 bars
* humanize (micro-timing) so that it sounds like a real drummer
* there is a "Feel" slider that can be adjusted from "thin" to "fat" (thin meaning very little going on and fat meaning many fills and variations and drive)
* feel will adjust automatically by the drummer to create tension and release
* there is a button "auto-fill" to enable and disable automatic fills
* "auto-feel" button to automatically control the feel slider  (or not)
* we have a tempo knob as well as a swing knob to adjust the tempo and swing of the groove
* The groove is always in 4/4, entered manually in a very simple form. Fills, variations, ghost notes and so on are generated on the fly by the drummeraw
